# LabLabee

> LabLabee is an AI-powered enablement, testing and troubleshooting platform for telecom teams —
> "the Copilot for Telco Engineers." It delivers on-demand hands-on labs, sandboxes, learning paths
> and skill tests across 4G/5G and Open RAN, IMS, network OAM, SDN/IP, telco cloud (OpenStack,
> OpenShift, Kubernetes, Docker), DevOps/GitOps automation, Linux, IAM and cybersecurity, and
> Industry 4.0/IoT — with AI Ask, AI Test and AI Troubleshoot layered on top. LabLabee does not
> publish a public API, developer portal, SDKs or CLI as of 2026-07-19.
